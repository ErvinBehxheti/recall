# Presentation day checklist

## The night before

- [ ] Charge the laptop and pack the charger.
- [ ] Run `npm run dev` once **with internet**, open http://localhost:3000 and press D. This downloads the fonts so the app looks right offline.
- [ ] Generate one real lesson from a real deck, so you know the AI works with your key.
- [ ] Turn Wi-Fi off and test demo mode: press D, go through two pages, answer one question. Turn Wi-Fi back on.
- [ ] Copy the promo video to the laptop, a USB stick, and a cloud folder.
- [ ] Put the opening screen, the video and the closing screen in your slides, in that order.
- [ ] Read through the pitch script out loud with a timer. Aim for 3:00 to 3:15.

## 10 minutes before

- [ ] Start the app: open a terminal in the project folder and run `npm run dev`.
- [ ] Open http://localhost:3000, press D once, then click the Slidekick logo to go back home.
- [ ] Press F11 for full screen.
- [ ] Turn on Do Not Disturb so no notifications pop up.
- [ ] Check the volume for the video, but don't depend on sound: the video works silently.
- [ ] Close every other app and browser tab.

## If something goes wrong on stage

- **The upload is slow or fails:** press D (or click "Try the sample lesson") and keep talking. The sample is a real lesson.
- **The app won't open at all:** skip the live demo and let the video show the product.
- **You run out of time:** skip the sentence marked "cut if we're behind" in the script.
